It Started With a Simple Question. Nutrizen began with a belief that better products shouldn't be difficult to access. RIVR is our first step in building a portfolio of healthier, hygienic and affordable everyday products.

“From our first brand, RIVR, to the products that will follow, we're building a company focused on making better everyday choices available to more people”

Why should better products be out of reach for everyday people?

Our founder envisioned a company that could create products with a stronger focus on health, hygiene, quality and affordability while keeping the needs of everyday consumers at the centre.

“We don't want to create products simply because there is a market for them. We want to create products because they can make everyday choices better.”

— Sagar Varma
